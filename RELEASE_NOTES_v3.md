# RoughCert-SPX v3.0.0

This release accompanies the working paper *RoughCert-SPX: Certified-Bias SPX Option Pricing and Kernel Audits under Correlated Rough Volatility*. The archive contains the modular article, the six experiment records, the independent verification programs and reproducible numerical exhibits.

The revision specifies the sampling assumptions for continuum envelopes and the exceptional strike in the formal leverage expansion. It attributes the variance-matched rDonsker coefficients to Eq. (4.2) of Horvath, Jacquier, Muguruza and Søjmark and identifies the alternative cell-mean coefficients. The two VIX result files from RoughCert v5.0.0 are bundled with pinned hashes. Figure 2 links the two plotted boundaries at a common Hurst exponent and Figure 6 uses shorter panel titles.

The package README gives the reproduction commands. The seven figures and the numerical tables can be regenerated from the archived results.
