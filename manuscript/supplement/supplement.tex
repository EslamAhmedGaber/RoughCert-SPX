\section{Refinement of the diagnostic reference strikes}
\label{sec:s_refinement}
The four reference envelopes with the most diagnostic flags (Regimes~A and~B of Experiment~E4 and the Pure~BDG6 and Mixed~Scen.~1 rows of Experiment~E6 at $\rho=-0.85$) were recomputed with the same path budgets, one more dyadic level and fresh seeds. Table~\ref{tab:s_refinement} compares the two runs. Of the $\RefFlagsVOne$ reference flags, $\RefUpgraded$ reach asymptotic-envelope status and $\RefNewFlags$ strike is newly flagged, leaving $\RefFlagsVTwo$ diagnostic strikes: $\RefSlowRemain$ call-wing strikes contract more slowly than $\beta_{\mathrm{FSW}}/2$ (fitted rates between $\RefRemainEmpMin$ and $\RefRemainEmpMax$) and $\RefEmergingRemain$ Pure~BDG6 strikes show an emerging increment. The remaining frontier therefore lies on the call side of the high-vol-of-vol E6 models, where the dyadic contraction has not reached its asymptotic rate by $n=\RefFinestLevel$. The refined Regime~A skew interval is $[\RefASkewLo,\RefASkewHi]$ and the Regime~B interval is $[\RefBSkewLo,\RefBSkewHi]$; the Regime~A ATM width falls to $\RefAATMWidthVTwo\bps$.

\section{Decomposition of the certifiability boundary}
\label{sec:s_decomposition}
Table~\ref{tab:s_e2_decomposition} reports, for each $H$, the $25\bps$ boundary of the two-moment bracket at $\rho=0$, the boundary of the first-order bracket of Theorem~\mref{thm:put_lipschitz} at $\rho=0$ and at $\rho=-0.9$, and the ratios between them. The total contraction factor of Experiment~E2 ($\ETwoCollapseMin$--$\ETwoCollapseMax$) is the product of a second-order factor ($\ETwoSecondOrderMin$--$\ETwoSecondOrderMax$), which measures the gain of the two-moment bound that exists only at $\rho=0$, and a leverage factor ($\ETwoLeverageMin$--$\ETwoLeverageMax$), which compares the same first-order bracket at the two correlations. The leverage factor hardly depends on $H$, as the first-order term of Theorem~\mref{thm:homotopy}(b) suggests.

\section{Theorem cases and the Case~(ii) rate floor}
\label{sec:s_case_ii}
Case~(ii) of Theorem~\mref{thm:bridge_envelope} applies when the finest increment is submerged in sampling noise; the envelope then uses the rate $\beta_{\mathrm{FSW}}(H)$. With safety factor $\gamma_{\mathrm{sf}}=\BridgeSafety$, the envelope still covers the continuum price when the increments decay geometrically at any true rate $\beta$ with $1/(2^\beta-1)\le\gamma_{\mathrm{sf}}/(2^{\beta_{\mathrm{FSW}}}-1)$, that is, for $\beta\ge\beta_{\min}(H)=\log_2(1+(2^{\beta_{\mathrm{FSW}}(H)}-1)/\gamma_{\mathrm{sf}})$. Rounded up, $\beta_{\min}=\BetaMinZeroFive$, $\BetaMinSeven$, $\BetaMinTen$ and $\BetaMinFifteen$ at $H=0.05$, $0.07$, $0.10$ and $0.15$, and $\beta_{\min}=\BetaMinSixth$ for $H\ge1/6$. Experiment~E5 supports the floor for every tested $H\ge0.10$ (smallest margin $\EFiveMinMargin$) but not at $H=0.05$, where the \RCCE{} strip-mean rate ($\EFiveStripCEHZeroFive$), the left-point strip-mean rate ($\EFiveStripLPHZeroFive$) and the \RCCE{} ATM rate ($\EFiveATMCEHZeroFive$) fall below $\BetaMinZeroFive$. Table~\ref{tab:s_case_counts} shows that Case~(ii) carries $\CaseTwoCount$ of the $\CaseTotalCount$ reference-envelope strikes.

\section{Exact Jensen deficit}
\label{sec:s_jensen}
Table~\ref{tab:s_jensen_deficit} gives the exact deficit $\delta_n$ of Proposition~\mref{prop:jensen_pitfall} in Regime~A, computed by Gaussian conditioning on the grid filtration with Gauss--Legendre quadrature of increasing order, together with the closed-form lower bound and the effective correlation $\rho_{\mathrm{eff}}$. The deficit decays like $n^{-2H}$ and is still above $\JenDefFiveTwelve\%$ at $n=512$.

\section{Verification summary}
\label{sec:s_checks}
Table~\ref{tab:s_checks} lists $\ProofChecksN$ first-principles checks of the stated results and $\IndepChecksN$ code-disjoint recomputations of archived numbers; neither script imports the production code. Check~I3 also confirms the implied-volatility mapping of the hybrid-kernel gaps, and check~I6 confirms that the E6 VIX inputs equal the archives of \RoughCert{} v5.0.0 bit for bit.
